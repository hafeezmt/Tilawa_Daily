# 📋 Tilawa Daily Changelog

All notable changes and milestones for the **Tilawa Daily** platform.

---

## 🚀 Version 2.0.0 — Major Milestone Release

### 🎨 Visual & Aesthetic Design
- **Clean White & Royal Gold Design System**: Refined 2-color harmonious palette (`#FFFFFF` + `#D97706` amber gold).
- **Zero Colored Shadows**: Elimination of all colored glows for clean, crisp, printed Mushaf typography.
- **Printed Mushaf Layout**: High-contrast black Quranic text with responsive font size slider (22px to 42px).

### 🎙️ Live Voice Halaqah Engine
- **Google Meet Multi-User Participant Grid**: Live audio waveform speaking indicators, role badges (*Ustadh / Reciter / Member*), and raised hand queue.
- **Waiting Room / Knock Admission**: Meeting codes with Ustadh floating admission controls (`[Admit]` / `[Deny]`).
- **Ustadh Remote Moderation**: Stop Recitation button and remote mute controls.
- **Web Audio Chimes**: Auditory alerts for hand raise and member admission.
- **Network Quality Monitor**: Sub-second latency diagnostics and HD audio indicators.

### 📖 Quran Mushaf & Reciter Suite
- **Multiple World-Class Qaris**: Dynamic reciter switcher for Mishary Alafasy, Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary, Abdulbasit Abdussamad, and Saud Al-Shuraim.
- **Floating Sticky Audio Player**: Stop, Play, Pause, Previous / Next Ayah controls with audio sync.
- **Hifz Memorization Loop**: Repeat iteration calculation engine for Ayah memorization.
- **Ayah Bookmarks**: Persistent local storage bookmark manager.
- **15 Ayat As-Sajdah Index**: Prostration verse markers across all 60 Hizbs.
- **Arabic Text Normalization**: Tashkeel-insensitive phonetic Quranic search filter.

### 🛡️ Admin & Khatm Tools
- **Khatm Progress Analytics**: 12-day 60-Hizb cycle estimation and countdown stats.
- **CSV Data Exporter**: 1-click Khatm recitation report generator for Ustadhs.
- **WhatsApp Invitation Generator**: Pre-formatted Hausa and English halaqah invite generator.
- **Khatm Diploma Generator**: Islamic completion certificate template.
- **Dua Khatm Al-Quran**: Authentic supplications in Arabic, Hausa, and English.
- **Daily Tadabbur Reflections**: Daily Quranic lessons and reflections.
- **Islamic Times & Hijri Calendar**: Session schedule and UmalQura date formatters.
- **Automated Unit Tests**: Metadata integrity and Ustadh PIN authorization test suites.

### 📱 Mobile & PWA
- **Progressive Web App**: Web App Manifest and mobile home-screen installable.
- **Native Android Framework**: Capacitor integration with microphone and background audio permissions.
- **Haptic Touch Engine**: Vibration feedback for tactile mobile interactions.
